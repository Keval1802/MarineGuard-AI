GULF OF KHAMBHAT OCEANOGRAPHY, TIDAL HYDRODYNAMICS & SENSITIVITY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1: Tidal Regimes & Estuarine Hydrodynamics
1. Semi-Diurnal Macro-Tides: The Gulf of Khambhat exhibits extreme macro-tidal ranges exceeding 8 to 10 meters, creating intense surface tidal currents reaching 2.5 to 3.5 m/s during spring flood/ebb cycles.
2. Tapi and Narmada River Plumes: Heavy monsoonal freshwater runoff carries suspended mud and silt into the Hazira approach channel. Satellite turbidity indices (Red/NIR ratio) frequently observe natural sediment plumes near Tapi river mouth, requiring cross-verification against floating material indices (NIR/SWIR ratio) and SAR wind gates.

Section 2: Sensitive Coastal Ecosystems & Impact Priorities
1. Intertidal Mangroves: Dense Avicennia marina mangrove habitats line the Tapi estuary, Suvali beach, and Dumas coast. Oil or toxic chemical landfalls cause severe root hypoxia and ecosystem degradation. Containment priority for slicks drifting towards 21.12°N - 21.18°N is CRITICAL.
2. Industrial Infrastructure & Port Intakes: Hazira Industrial Complex houses major LNG terminals, power plants, and chemical processing facilities with seawater cooling intakes. Floating debris or oil entering cooling water intakes causes emergency shutdown.

Section 3: Trajectory Simulation Vector Calibration
1. Surface Drift Physics: Oil and floating waste drift is calculated using 3.5% windage coefficient (alpha = 0.035) with a 15-degree Coriolis deflection, combined with 100% surface current vector velocity (beta = 1.00).
