MARITIME POLLUTION RESPONSE PROCEDURE & SENSITIVITY MANUAL
------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1: Oil Spill Anomaly Response Protocols
When an oil-like surface anomaly is detected via Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) or optical satellite imagery:
1. Verification & Wind Window Assessment: Verify local sea-surface wind speeds. Dark SAR patches observed in wind speeds between 2 m/s and 10 m/s indicate a high-confidence oil sheen candidate. Wind speeds below 2 m/s frequently generate biogenic slick look-alikes, while wind speeds exceeding 10 m/s lead to rapid natural dispersion.
2. Containment Strategy: For high-priority slicks threatening coastal mangroves or port channels, deploy oil containment booms around the seaward perimeter. Skimming vessels should be dispatched if estimated slick thickness exceeds 0.1 mm.
3. Dispersant Guidelines: Chemical dispersants are strictly prohibited in shallow estuarine waters (< 10 m depth) such as the Tapi and Mindhola river outlets due to severe benthic toxicity.

Section 2: Floating Waste & Plastic Accumulation
1. Debris Patch Monitoring: Floating material candidates identified via elevated NIR/SWIR reflectance ratios should be tracked for coastal landfall.
2. Recovery Protocols: Floating debris barriers should be established across narrow tidal creek mouths before high tide flood cycles to prevent debris from washing deep into intertidal mangrove roots.
